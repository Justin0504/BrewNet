## Common questions

- **How does Brew set up coffee chats?** Tell Brew who you'd like to meet; it finds fits, drafts the intro, and — when both sides say yes — books the coffee.
- **How do I delete my account?** Profile tab → top-right menu (•••) → Delete Account.
- **BrewNet Pro** unlocks unlimited scouting and more weekly picks. Founding members get Pro free with an invite code.
