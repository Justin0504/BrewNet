BrewNet Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

BrewNet ("we", "our", or "us") operates the BrewNet mobile application (the "App"), an AI-assisted professional networking service. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name and email address (or Apple ID relay email if you use Sign in with Apple), used to create and secure your account.
  • Profile information: details you choose to add — role, company, school, skills, bio, availability, and photos you upload.
  • Content you create: coffee-chat invitations, messages, and introductions you send through the App.
  • Location (optional): approximate/precise location, only if you grant permission, to suggest nearby people and coffee venues.
  • Usage data: how you interact with features (e.g., matches shown, invitations sent), used to improve matching and the product.
  • Payment information: if you subscribe to BrewNet Pro, purchases are processed by Apple; we receive subscription status, not your card details.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: create and operate your account; power AI matching and introductions; deliver messages, invitations, and notifications; provide and improve features; and maintain safety and security. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

AI Processing

To generate matches, introduction drafts, and briefings, relevant profile text may be processed by third-party large-language-model providers (e.g., Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) acting as our service providers. We share only what is needed to produce the result and do not permit them to use your data to train their models beyond providing the service.

Sharing

We share information only: with other users as part of the networking features you initiate (e.g., an introduction you send); with service providers who help us operate the App (hosting, AI, push delivery) under confidentiality obligations; or when required by law.

Data Storage & Security

Data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase). We apply industry-standard safeguards, including on-device data protection and encrypted transport (HTTPS).

Your Choices & Rights

  • Access & edit: update your profile anytime in the App.
  • Delete your account: Profile tab → menu (•••) → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, profile, matches, and messages.
  • Permissions: you can grant or revoke location, photos, and notifications in iOS Settings.

Children

BrewNet is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
